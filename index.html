Before you begin

This session is built on a simple premise: the person sitting next to you has already solved a problem you're facing. Every activity below is a structured way to trade real, usable strategies — not just vent about a hard week.

⏱ 60 min 👥 Works solo-pairs-groups of 4 🎯 4 rounds
Setup: 5 labeled stations for the Strategy Swap Board (Classroom Management, Grading & Feedback, Family Communication, Documentation, Intervention & Support), sticky notes, chart paper, a visible clock or this page's timers projected.

Tip: In the Case Clinic, hold the group to questions-only before advice — most first-year teachers jump straight to fixing, and the questioning phase is where the real thinking happens.

Personal reference & log

Keep the paperwork from piling up

Six categories most new teachers are expected to keep records of. Open any category for a quick habit checklist, and log entries as they happen so you're never reconstructing a timeline from memory later.

Entries are saved privately to this browser only — treat this as a personal working log, not your school's official system of record.

MTSS / RTI thinking tool

Organize your next step

Answer four quick questions about a student's response to a current intervention. You'll get a suggested next step and a set of prep notes to bring to your student support team — this tool helps you think it through, it doesn't replace your team's decision.

1. What tier is the student currently receiving support at?

2. How long has this specific intervention been in place?

3. How many data points have you collected on this skill or behavior?

4. Based on your data, how is the student responding?

Prep notes for your team meeting:

    This suggestion is a starting point for your own thinking and team conversation, not a formal MTSS/RTI or special education determination.
